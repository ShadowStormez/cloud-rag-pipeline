Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Ludex is an AI-powered game-development documentation assistant operated as an independent educational software project. For privacy questions, contact atehrani@uef.fi.

1. Information we process

When you use Ludex, we process the questions you submit, your selected game engine, and the answers generated in response. Questions may contain information you choose to provide, so please avoid submitting passwords, API keys, or other sensitive information.

If you sign in using Google, authentication is handled through Google and Supabase. We may receive account information such as your email address, display name, and account identifier.

2. Guest conversations

You can ask questions without signing in. Guest conversation history is maintained temporarily in the application's memory and is not saved to the Ludex conversation database. Refreshing or closing the page may erase your guest conversations. Your questions are still sent to the backend and AI provider for processing.

3. Signed-in conversation history

If you sign in, Ludex stores your conversation titles, messages, generated responses, and documentation references using Supabase so you can return to your previous conversations.

4. AI processing and service providers

Ludex uses an API hosted on Microsoft Azure and an AI model accessed through Groq to generate responses. Your questions and relevant documentation excerpts may be transmitted to these services for processing. Supabase provides authentication and conversation storage; Google provides optional account sign-in; Cloudflare hosts the website.

These providers may process technical and usage information according to their applicable privacy terms. Ludex does not control every aspect of third-party infrastructure logging or retention.

5. Purpose and legal basis

We process information to provide requested AI answers, authenticate users who choose to sign in, maintain saved conversations, and operate and secure the service. Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may be necessary to provide the requested service or based on legitimate interests in security and service operation.

6. Retention and deletion

Guest chat history is temporary in the browser application. Signed-in conversation records are currently retained until deleted through an operator-assisted request or a future in-app deletion feature. Third-party providers may have separate retention periods.

7. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside Finland or the European Economic Area. Applicable safeguards and provider terms govern such processing.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email atehrani@uef.fi. You may also have the right to complain to a relevant data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Ludex evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
